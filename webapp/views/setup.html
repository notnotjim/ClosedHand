  4. 04

    Connect the account that holds your mail and calendar. Either works, and you can add the other later.

    Google connected

    Don’t trade your digital sovereignty for an easier setup. Five extra minutes now gives you ownership of the connection, rather than handing it to a tech conglomerate.

    Why the extra Google setup?

    ClosedHand uses Google’s usual sign-in, through a free project you own. This one-time setup keeps the connection to your mail, calendar and Drive under your control, with no company account in between.

    Connected. ClosedHand reads this account's mail, calendar and Drive so it can recall them, and changes nothing unless you ask. Other Google accounts can be added later from the dashboard.

    Manage in the dashboard Connect a different account
    Step 1 of 5

    Google opens in a new tab. Return here after each step; the final sign-in brings you back automatically.

    Create your project

    Google keeps the access in a free project that only you can use and can delete later. Click the button below and name the project anything you like. Leave the second field as 'No Organization'.

    Important: before you click Create on the project screen, copy the Project ID shown under the name you chose (often it's the same as the name you chose, sometimes with numbers added). This is what goes into the box below.

    Open the New Project screen Where the ID is

    Switch on the services

    Enable Gmail, Calendar and Drive together. Click the button below and on the screen that appears, click Next, then Enable. Once the permissions are enabled, come back here and move to the next step.

    Switch on the three services

    Introduce the app

    Fill out the form by clicking the button below and putting the following in each field:

    1. App name: ClosedHand
    2. User support email: choose your email address from the dropdown
    3. Audience: External. This won't share anything with anyone: it is Google's word for an app that ordinary Google accounts like yours can sign in to, rather than one locked to a company's workspace.
    4. Contact information: your email address
    5. Finish: tick to agree, then click Create

    Then come back here and move to the next step.

    Open the form

    Make the sign-in key

    Open the Clients page below and click + Create client. Choose Web application from the dropdown (you can leave the Name field as is). Under Authorized redirect URIs, paste this line exactly:

    http://localhost:3000/auth/google/callback
    Open the Clients page

    Important: press Create, then click Download JSON before closing the dialog. Google shows that file only once; if you miss it, create another client.

    Where Download JSON is

    Then hand ClosedHand the file you downloaded. Pick it here, or drop it on the box, or open it and paste the contents.

    Publish the app and sign in

    While the app stays in "Testing", Google makes you sign in again every seven days, so it needs publishing. Open the Branding page below, scroll down to App domain, and fill in:

    1. Application home page: https://closedhand.com
    2. Application privacy policy link: https://closedhand.com/privacy
    3. Authorized domains: click + Add domain and type closedhand.com
    4. Click Save
    Open the Branding page

    Then open the Audience page below, click Publish app and Confirm. Google’s wording means others can authorise their own data, not access yours or your dashboard. Personal use needs no verification.

    Open the Audience page

    Sign in with the Google account whose mail, calendar and Drive you want to connect. It can differ from the account that owns your project. Google will warn that the app is unverified, because it is your private one. Choose Advanced, then Go to ClosedHand, then Continue. On the permissions screen, tick Select all, then Continue. ClosedHand needs all of them.

    Sign in with Google

    The sign-in button appears once the file from step four is saved.
