Save it on your iPhone

Open your dashboard in Safari first if you’re viewing it inside a chat app.

  1. Tap Safari’s Share button. You may find it in the menu beside the address.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Confirm with Add. ClosedHand will appear on your home screen.

Save it on your phone

Scan the code above with your phone’s camera, then sign in with your dashboard password.

On iPhone, open your dashboard in Safari, tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in your browser and look for Install app or Add to Home screen in the menu.

Bookmark it or pin it in your chat

Bookmark the dashboard in your browser, or pin the message containing its link in WhatsApp or Telegram. You can keep using the same address.

Your computer must be awake and online. Your dashboard password is still required.
