Setup · Google

Your Google, answering only to you.

Other apps reach your Google data through their own company. ClosedHand has no company in between, so Google needs you to set this up yourself. Six small steps, and every one opens the right page for you.

Less than it looks. Google keeps that permission in a container it calls a project: nothing more than the box your key lives in. It is free, only you can use it, and you can delete it later. Six small steps, one action each, and the steps use Google's own words so your screen always matches this page.

Google is connected.

ClosedHand is syncing your mail and calendar in the background. Give it a few minutes, then ask your inbox something from the dashboard.

  1. 01

    Create your project

    This screen only creates new projects, so nothing you already have can be touched. Name it anything and press Create.

    Open the New Project screen

    The ID appears under the name field while you create it, and in the console's top bar afterwards. Pasting it unlocks the steps below, each opening in the right project.

  2. 02

    Switch on the services

    One screen enables Gmail, Calendar and Drive together. It names your new project at the top; press Next, then Enable.

    Paste your project id into step 01 to open this.

    Switch on the three services
  3. 03

    Introduce the app

    A short form, a few quick fields: app name ClosedHand, your own email for the contact fields, and choose External when asked. Agree to Google's data policy on the last screen and press Create.

    Paste your project id into step 01 to open this.

    Open the form
  4. 04

    Make the sign-in key

    Press Create client, choose Web application, and under Authorized redirect URIs add exactly this line:

    http://localhost:3000/auth/google/callback

    Paste your project id into step 01 to open this.

    Open the Clients page
    Press Create, then click Download JSON before closing the dialog. Google shows this file only once. If you miss it, create another client.
  5. 05

    Publish the app

    While an app stays in Testing, Google expires its keys every seven days and ClosedHand would ask you to sign in again each week. Press Publish app and confirm. Your project still serves only you; publishing just stops the weekly expiry.

    Paste your project id into step 01 to open this.

    Open the Audience page

    When you sign in later, Google shows a "hasn't verified this app" warning. That is its boilerplate for any personal project: choose Advanced, then Go to your app's name, and carry on.

  6. 06

    Hand ClosedHand the key

    Open the JSON file you downloaded and paste all of it here. ClosedHand reads the two values it needs from it.

    Connect Google

    After saving, press Connect. Google will warn that the app is unverified; that is because it is your private app. Click Continue. When the connection completes, this page updates and the first sync begins.
