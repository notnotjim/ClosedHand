Wipe all data
Deletes conversations, Context Notes, pinned facts, rules, schedules, datasets, automations, and indexed files. Your profile and connected services stay, and mail and calendar keep syncing from them.

Delete account
Permanently remove your account and all associated data. This cannot be undone.
This permanently deletes your entire account and every piece of stored data, including the synced copies of your mail and calendar. You will be logged out immediately. This cannot be undone.
