Open on the computer where ClosedHand is installed

Mac app: Launch ClosedHand from Applications. Then click its menu bar icon and choose Open ClosedHand.

Docker: Start Docker and your ClosedHand containers first. Then open http://localhost:3000 on that computer. If you changed the port, use that port instead.

If Docker runs on a remote server, start ClosedHand there and forward port 3000 over SSH before opening the local link.

To open ClosedHand from any device, choose a personal URL in Settings → Personal URL.
